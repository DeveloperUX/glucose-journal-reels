Why does your mouth fill with spit right before you puke? 🤢💦

Your body is protecting you:
🧠 Your brain senses vomit coming and cranks up your spit glands
🔥 Stomach acid is strong enough to eat away tooth enamel
💧 That flood of saliva is packed with bicarbonate, a built-in antacid
🛡️ It coats your teeth and throat before the acid hits

Pro tip: after throwing up, rinse with water and wait 30 to 60 min before brushing. Brushing right away scrubs acid into softened enamel.
