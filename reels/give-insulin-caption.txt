Your friend with type 1 diabetes passes out. Do you give them insulin? ❌ NO.

Here's what to actually do:
🔻 Passing out is usually a LOW: too little sugar, not too much
🧃 Awake and can swallow? Fast sugar, like juice or regular soda
🚫 Not awake? Nothing in their mouth (choking risk)
🚨 Use their glucagon if they have it, and call 911

Insulin lowers blood sugar. Giving it during a low can be deadly.
