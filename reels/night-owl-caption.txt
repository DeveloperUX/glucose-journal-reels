Wide awake at 1 AM and a zombie at 7? You're not lazy. It's in your genes 🦉🧬

🧬 A study of ~700,000 people found 350+ spots in our DNA that help set the body clock
🌙 About 1 in 3 people lean night owl
⏱️ Some carry a tiny gene change that makes their inner clock run slow
🧒 Puberty pushes melatonin (the sleep hormone) up to ~2 hours later, so 11 PM can feel like 9 PM
✈️ Owls forced to wake early live in "social jet lag", linked to more stress and a higher risk of type 2 diabetes

What helps:
☀️ Bright light right after you wake
📱 Dim screens at night
⏰ Same wake time, even on weekends
You can nudge your clock. You can't delete it.
